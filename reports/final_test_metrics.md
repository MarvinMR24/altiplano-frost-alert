Generated by `pipelines/06_final_eval.py --i-confirm-final`. Test years 2018-2025, 23376 labeled nights over 8 cells. Every model, threshold and fitted statistic was frozen on validation (2010-2017) before this run; nothing was re-tuned on test. The 2026 out-of-sample nights are not part of any metric.
